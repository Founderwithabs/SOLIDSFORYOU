# Motion taste notes

What this user likes and dislikes in motion graphics, learned from real
videos. The motion-design-taste skill reads this before every review, and
these notes override its general rules when they conflict.

Add notes as measured facts, not adjectives ("cards enter in 10 frames with
an ease-out from 40 px below", not "smooth cards"). Format:

```
## <video name> (<liked | disliked>, <YYYY-MM-DD>)
Source: <file path or link>
- <measured observation>
- <what makes it work or fail>
```
