# Motion taste notes

What this user likes and dislikes in motion graphics, learned from real
videos. The motion-design-taste skill reads this before every review, and
these notes override its general rules when they conflict.

Add notes as measured facts, not adjectives ("cards enter in 10 frames with
an ease-out from 40 px below", not "smooth cards"). Format:

```
## <video name> (<liked | disliked>, <YYYY-MM-DD>)
Source: <file path or link>
- <measured observation>
- <what makes it work or fail>
```

## SFY reference reel, SFY_Reel_Final_v2.mp4 (liked, 2026-10-22)
Source: described in the LRDI video handoff; not yet measured frame by frame.
Inspect it with the skill and replace these with measured notes.
- Dark navy/black background with a deep blue glow.
- Floating cards, small spaced-caps labels, big numerals, progress rings.
- Fast card swaps.
- Strictly 2D motion graphics; no 3D.
- Every spoken line gets its own visual change.
- On-screen text is key phrases only, not subtitles.

## SOLIDS FOR YOU launch video v1, examples/launch-video (disliked, 2026-10-24)
Source: the first launch video (32 s, 1920x1080); founder's verdict: "all of these motions look the same, all of the cards look the same, the background feels very childish."
- Every element entered the same way: a 12-14 frame ease-out slide from 120 px right or 40 px below, plus a fade. Nine scenes, one move. Vary the entrance per scene (masked text reveals, lines drawing, bars growing, wipes, hard cuts on the beat).
- Five tool scenes used one identical layout (label, prompt, text card, ring with a number). Each tool needs its own visual that shows what it does (a marked-up listing, a price chart, a ledger, a phone post, an edit timeline), not the same card with new words.
- A big soft blue radial glow on navy read as childish. Prefer a near-black or paper background with no glow blobs; structure from thin lines, a grid, type and one accent colour.
- This overrides the "deep blue glow" and "floating cards" notes in the SFY reference reel entry above.

## SOLIDS FOR YOU website redesign (disliked details, 2026-10-26)
Source: founder's feedback on the mobile-first homepage preview.
- Text placed on top of product or model photos (a "Solid Blue · M" tag in a black box, "01/03" counters, labels over the hero) makes the photos look fake. Keep photos clean: labels, prices and headlines go above or below the image, never on it, and the header doesn't float over a photo.
- Never use the word "real" in copy ("Real photo. Real fabric.", "Yes, really.").
- Assets come only from the brand's own Drive folder (product, model, street and crew photos), not from older site files or the Rainbrand folder.
