# Marketplace guide

What to know about each marketplace before listing, pricing or reviewing
on it. This guide names **what to check**, not the current numbers: fees,
limits and rules change often and vary by country and category. Always
confirm them on the marketplace's own seller help pages or rate card (or
the founder's settlement report) and record the date. Where a well-known
figure is given, it is marked "check" and must be confirmed before use.

## How to use this guide

For the marketplace in the task:
1. Read its section below.
2. Check the current category rules, image rules and fees on its seller
   site, and note the URL and date.
3. Build or update the fee profile (`modules/economics.md`).
4. Then follow the module for the task.

For a marketplace not listed here, find the same things: listing fields and
limits, image rules, fee components, fulfilment options, programmes that
affect visibility, review rules, and tax and legal requirements.

## Amazon (all regions: US, CA, MX, BR, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, SE, PL, IN, JP, AU, AE, SA, SG and others)

- **Listing fields:** title, bullet points (usually five), description,
  backend search terms (limited in bytes; check), item attributes, variation
  themes (parent and child). A+ content and Brand Stores need Brand Registry.
  Title rules have been tightening (shorter limits in many categories,
  limits on repeated words and special characters); check the category
  style guide.
- **Images:** main image on a pure white background (RGB 255), product only,
  no added text, logos or watermarks, filling most of the frame (check the
  current percentage); long side large enough to enable zoom (check, often
  1,000 px or more; 2,000 px is a safe target). Up to several more images
  and video.
- **Fees to put in the profile:** referral fee (a percentage, often tiered by
  price and category), closing or fixed fees in some regions, fulfilment
  fees (FBA) or seller-fulfilled shipping, storage fees (monthly, long-term
  and peak season), returns processing in some categories, subscription fee
  per month, and tax on fees where it applies (for example GST in India, VAT
  in the UK and EU).
- **Visibility:** the featured offer (Buy Box), Prime or fast-delivery
  badges, Amazon's Choice and Best Seller badges, sponsored ads, deals and
  coupons, and the "bought in past month" signal.
- **Reviews:** only through Request a Review, Vine and other official
  programmes. Incentivised or manipulated reviews break policy.
- **Watch:** account health metrics, restricted categories and products,
  invoices or brand authorisation for some brands, and country-specific
  product safety and labelling rules (for example GPSR in the EU, legal
  metrology declarations in India).

## Walmart Marketplace (US, CA, MX, CL)

- **Listing fields:** product name, key features (bullets), description,
  attributes; variant groups. Brand Portal for brand owners; rich media for
  enhanced content.
- **Images:** white-background main image and high-resolution requirements
  (check the current minimum size).
- **Fees:** referral fee by category (no monthly subscription in the US
  marketplace; check), Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) fees if used.
- **Visibility:** the Buy Box, fast-shipping tags, WFS, Sponsored Products,
  and the listing quality score in Seller Center.
- **Watch:** seller performance standards, approval to sell, pricing rules
  that unpublish items priced far above other retailers.

## eBay (all regions)

- **Listing fields:** title (check the character limit, often 80), item
  specifics (very important for search and filters), condition, description,
  variations, Best Offer settings.
- **Images:** several photos allowed; minimum size and no borders or added
  text on the main photo (check).
- **Fees:** insertion fees beyond free allowances, final value fee (a
  percentage of the total including shipping, by category), per-order fee,
  promoted listings fees, store subscription if used, international fees.
- **Visibility:** Best Match ranking, item specifics completeness, shipping
  speed and cost, seller level (Top Rated), Promoted Listings.

## Etsy

- **Listing fields:** title (check the character limit), up to 13 tags (check
  length), attributes, categories, description, personalisation and
  variations. Handmade, vintage and craft-supply rules apply; production
  partners must be disclosed.
- **Images:** several photos and a video; the first photo is the thumbnail
  and is often cropped; check the recommended ratio and size.
- **Fees:** listing fee, transaction fee (on item price plus shipping and
  gift wrap), payment processing fee by country, offsite ads fee on sales
  from Etsy's ads (can be mandatory above a sales threshold; check),
  currency conversion.
- **Visibility:** search relevance (title, tags, attributes), listing
  quality, shipping price and speed, reviews, star seller status.

## TikTok Shop (US, UK, SEA and other regions)

- **Listing fields:** product title, description, attributes, variants,
  size charts for apparel.
- **Images:** square images and video; check minimum size and rules on text.
- **Fees:** referral or commission fee by category, payment fee where
  separate, affiliate creator commission you set, fulfilment fees if using
  the platform's logistics, ads.
- **Visibility:** content drives sales: short videos, LIVE selling and
  affiliate creators. Plan creator samples and commissions in the economics.
  Product review and shipping-performance rules are strict.

## Own store (Shopify, WooCommerce and others)

- **Product page:** title, description, variants, structured data for search,
  SEO title and meta description, reviews (from real buyers), shipping and
  returns information near the buy button, trust signals.
- **Images:** no marketplace rules, but the same taste rules apply; keep a
  consistent set and fast-loading files.
- **Costs to put in the profile:** payment gateway percentage and fixed fee,
  platform subscription and apps (spread per order), shipping partner rates,
  cash-on-delivery charges and RTO (returned, undelivered COD orders) costs
  where COD is common, packaging, customer acquisition cost (ads ÷ orders).
- **Watch:** conversion rate, checkout drop-off, page speed, return-to-origin
  rate for COD.

## Flipkart (India)

- **Listing fields:** title built from attributes, key features, description,
  many category attributes; catalogue quality affects visibility.
- **Images:** white background for the main image in most categories, size
  and count rules (check), model images for fashion.
- **Fees to put in the profile:** commission fee by category and price band,
  fixed fee, collection or payment fee, shipping fee by weight and zone
  (local, zonal, national), reverse shipping on returns, GST on these fees,
  and TCS collected by the marketplace (check rates).
- **Visibility:** Flipkart Assured or similar fulfilment badges, ratings,
  price competitiveness, ads, Big Billion Days and other sale events.
- **Watch:** high return rates in fashion, size charts, and Legal Metrology
  declarations on listings (MRP, net quantity, manufacturer and importer
  details, country of origin).

## Meesho (India)

- **Model:** reseller and value-focused buyers, very price-sensitive,
  mostly smaller towns. The commission model has changed over time (check
  the current one; it has advertised zero commission).
- **Listing:** catalogues with several images and simple attributes; the
  first image and price drive the click.
- **Costs:** shipping (often built into the price), returns and RTO (high in
  some categories), any platform fees, GST and TCS.
- **Watch:** return and quality ratings; a low price is necessary but the
  economics must still work after returns.

## Myntra and Ajio (India, fashion)

- **Model:** fashion and lifestyle only; brands are onboarded; listings are
  catalogued to strict standards, with model shots, size charts and
  attributes.
- **Fees:** commission, fixed or logistics fees, returns (high in fashion),
  and the business model (marketplace or outright purchase); check with the
  brand's account manager.
- **Watch:** size-chart accuracy, consistent model photography, return rate,
  and participation terms for end-of-season sales.

## Nykaa (India, beauty and personal care)

- **Model:** beauty, personal care and fashion; brand onboarding.
- **Watch:** ingredient lists, regulatory and labelling requirements for
  cosmetics, claims that need evidence ("dermatologically tested",
  "organic"), and expiry dates.

## Shopee and Lazada (Southeast Asia, Taiwan, Latin America for Shopee)

- **Listing:** title, description, attributes, variations; local language.
- **Fees:** commission, transaction or payment fee, programme fees (free
  shipping, cashback or voucher programmes) that can add several percent;
  check each country.
- **Visibility:** vouchers, flash sales, campaign days (for example 9.9,
  11.11, 12.12), live selling, preferred or mall seller status, chat
  response rate.

## Mercado Libre (Latin America)

- **Listing:** title, attributes (very important), description, variations;
  catalogue listings compete for the buy box.
- **Fees:** sale commission by listing type (classic or premium) and
  category, fixed fee below a price threshold, shipping (Mercado Envíos),
  Full fulfilment fees, instalment financing costs on premium listings.
- **Visibility:** seller reputation (the colour thermometer), Full
  fulfilment, free shipping, Mercado Ads.

## Noon (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt)

- **Listing:** English and Arabic content; title, highlights, specifications.
- **Fees:** referral fee by category, fulfilment fees (Fulfilled by Noon),
  VAT on fees.
- **Visibility:** express delivery badges, deals, Ramadan and White Friday
  events.

## Zalando (Europe, fashion)

- **Model:** fashion only, partner programme with Zalando's content
  standards; returns are frequent in fashion.
- **Watch:** size and fit data, consistent imagery, return costs in the
  economics, EU product safety (GPSR) and textile labelling.

## Allegro (Poland and Central Europe), Rakuten (Japan) and others

- Allegro: commission by category, Smart! delivery programme, seller rating;
  Polish-language content.
- Rakuten Ichiba: store-based model with monthly plan fees, system usage
  fees and points programmes; Japanese-language content and detailed pages.
- For any other marketplace, use the checklist in "How to use this guide".

## Tax and law reminders by region (inform, then refer to an advisor)

- **India:** prices are GST-inclusive; marketplaces collect TCS; GST applies
  to marketplace fees and is usually claimable as input credit; Legal
  Metrology rules require listing declarations; BIS certification is
  mandatory for some products.
- **EU and UK:** prices include VAT; marketplaces may collect VAT on some
  sales; EU GPSR requires a responsible person and safety information for
  many products; WEEE and packaging rules in some countries.
- **United States:** prices exclude sales tax, which marketplaces usually
  collect as marketplace facilitators; state-level rules vary; product safety
  (CPSC) and FTC rules on claims and "Made in USA".
- **Everywhere:** claims about health, the environment, origin and
  certifications need evidence; restricted categories need approval.
